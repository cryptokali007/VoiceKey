## Floating dictation

Click your destination text field, hold **Hold to dictate** with the mouse, speak, and release. VoiceKey keeps the caret in your app and pastes the text when local transcription finishes.

- **Dictation widget** opens a small always-on-top window. **Full app** brings back the WAV recorder, model settings, and transcript editor.
- **Set shortcut** lets you choose a key or a key combination. New installations start with semicolon on a US keyboard. Tap it to type punctuation, or hold it to dictate. Existing installations keep their saved shortcut.
- If you change windows during transcription, the words are available on the clipboard.

Download **VoiceKey-Windows-x64.zip**, extract it, close any running copy of VoiceKey, and double-click **Install.cmd**. Existing models, recordings, and preferences stay on your PC. For portable use, run **VoiceKey.exe** from the extracted folder.
